Infection 2013: original bitmap font notices
==========================================

The shipped data/dejavu16x16_gs_tc.png is the game's original 13360-byte
512 by 128 bitmap, SHA256:
cb17ade2c0a346af0de6f5d66877d68cf2736d1689ae7bc4dc4e34382e1720ce

It uses the TCOD layout (32 columns by 8 rows, 16 by 16 pixels per glyph).
It is not an ASCII-in-column font.  Comparison with the libtcod 1.5.1
sample at commit a7cabfda0b0c770d4092f0dec02efbd2a3b6d990 found 237
changed pixels in two glyph cells, (0,1) and (13,1); the other 254 cells
are identical.  The sample is not substituted for the author's bitmap.
The author's modifications are covered by the Luca Carbone BSD-2-Clause
notice installed as license.txt.  The title image and all game data also
remain the original archive files, not reconstructed assets.

DejaVu's lineage statement and both full permission notices follow, from
https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html (retrieved 2026-10-24).
The Arev notice is retained conservatively, without asserting a precise
per-glyph Arev inventory.  Public domain applies to DejaVu's changes,
not to the entire font.  The reserved-name conditions below remain in
force; dejavu16x16_gs_tc.png contains none of the reserved names.

libtcod bitmap distribution provenance
-------------------------------------

The original data/fonts/README.txt from libtcod 1.5.1 is installed
separately as libtcod-fonts-README.txt, from:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/libtcod/libtcod/a7cabfda0b0c770d4092f0dec02efbd2a3b6d990/data/fonts/README.txt

Its general statement "These fonts are in public domain" is preserved as
historical documentation, not used to override the specific Bitstream
Vera and Arev notices above.  The renderer's full BSD-3-Clause license,
credits, historical README-SDL and LodePNG codec sources/notices are
installed alongside this file.  README-SDL concerns the old SDL 1.2
bundling, not the licensing of the current Guix sdl12-compat/SDL2 runtime.
