FOR original active font: DejaVu raster atlas

The original bin/font.png is preserved byte for byte (SHA256
1e7af4dbdc86fd28e5c42d28dd892ff2f765da2d8f7272919820863fba1b8cce).
Its decoded pixels match the bundled libtcod 1.5.1
data/fonts/dejavu_wide16x16_gs_tc.png atlas.  libtcod's font README and
LIBTCOD-CREDITS.txt are installed alongside this notice unchanged.  The latter
credits Mingos for dejavu 10x10/12x12 bitmap fonts, not specifically this
16x16 wide atlas; its rasterizer is not identified there.  The README's
blanket public-domain claim does not override the underlying Bitstream and
Arev font notices below.
The DejaVu changes are public domain, not the entire underlying font.
