Six Two One, original 7DRL release (2016-03-06)
================================================
Canonical source: http://www.zincland.com/7drl/sixtwoone/sixtwoone7drl.zip
SHA-256: c3597321994f25a092e2b66cbeee0bf044331af4961bc434ec688770a63d6843

Game C++ source, generated enums, configuration and text: Jeff Lait,
BSD-3-Clause.  The embedded mt19937ar.c is copyright Makoto Matsumoto and
Takuji Nishimura under its three-clause BSD terms.  The complete notices
for both are reproduced in LICENSE.TXT.

rooms/village.map and wordlist/wordlist.txt (Moby Project): public domain,
as explicitly declared in LICENSE.TXT.

linux/terminal.png is the game's Oxygen Mono glyph image (despite the
.png suffix it has a BMP header).  Oxygen Mono is copyright 2012 Vernon
Adams, with Reserved Font Name Oxygen.  Its SIL Open Font License 1.1 and
copyright notice are reproduced in OFL.txt.  No other libtcod demo fonts
are installed.

libtcod 1.5.0, rebuilt from the same archive and linked into the game:
copyright 2008,2009,2010 Jice, BSD-3-Clause.  See LIBTCOD-LICENSE.txt and
LIBTCOD-CREDITS.txt.  No upstream executable/shared library is reused.

The game links dynamically to separately packaged Guix SDL 1.2 compatibility,
libpng and zlib libraries.  SDL12-compat and its SDL2 backend use the zlib
license; libpng uses the libpng license; zlib uses the zlib license.  Their
source and licenses are provided by their respective Guix packages.  The
historic LGPL SDL redistribution notice from the archive is retained as
README-SDL.txt and in LICENSE.TXT; it describes the old upstream binaries,
not the replacement SDL12-compat shipped by Guix.

Audio is disabled in upstream main.cpp (USE_AUDIO is commented out).
SDL_mixer and the unused music directory are not linked or redistributed.
Windows DLLs, opaque Linux executables, bundled SDL/libtcod shared libraries,
UPX tooling, demos and their data/font assets are not installed.
